# Dataset Sharing

This guide covers creating and managing dataset shares.

## Create a share

1. Open `Curated Datasets (List)` and click **Share** for a finalized dataset.
2. Enter:
   - Purpose
   - Created For
   - Recipient email (optional)
   - Expiry window (hours)
3. Submit to generate a share link and OTP.

## Distribute the share

- Share the download link with the recipient.
- Share the OTP separately (different channel).
- The recipient email receives the link (no OTP).
- The creator email receives the OTP (CC main_admin).

## Manage shares

- Multiple shares can be active at once.
- Use **Activate/Deactivate** to control access without deleting a share.
- Use **Regenerate OTP** to invalidate a previous OTP and issue a new one.

## Notes

- Deactivated shares remain listed but cannot download.
- OTPs are case-insensitive and time-bound.
