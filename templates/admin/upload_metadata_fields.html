Manage reusable patient, encounter, image, document, and upload-level metadata field masters.

EncounterSet Types
Patient
Participant-level metadata that can persist across visits, such as UHID/MRN, sex, age, phone, or email.
Encounter
One visit/capture session for one patient, such as capture date, diagnosis known at upload, clinic ID, or remarks.
Image
Per-image metadata such as eye, view, gaze, modality, distance/close-up, montage, disk-centered, or macula-centered.
Document
Supporting documents and document-images, usually PII, such as referrals, reports, PDFs, labels, or consent images.
Upload
Metadata about the upload event or batch itself, such as source system, operator notes, import batch, or acquisition method.
