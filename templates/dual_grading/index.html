How the Dual Grading System Works
  • Each glaucoma image must be graded by both a resident and a consultant
  • Residents are identified by the 'optometrist' role
  • Consultants are identified by the 'ophthalmologist' role
  • Admin users can grade as either role
  • The system tracks which images have been graded by which role
  • Discrepancies between resident and consultant gradings are highlighted for review
