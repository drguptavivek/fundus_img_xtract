Workbook sheets

  • Encounters: capture date, non-PII EncounterSet metadata, verification, image/task/grade counts, and completion.
  • Images: encounter UUID, image UUID, exported filename, position, and review state.
  • Tasks: every encounter and image task, including partially graded tasks.
  • Grade Submissions: every saved grading role and selected class features.
  • Final Grades: persisted consensus grade and method.

Image exports are limited to 250 matched EncounterSets per request. Narrow the capture date range if the preview exceeds the limit.
